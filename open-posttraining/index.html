Overview

Post-training as one extensible system: generate and execute agent rollouts, verify outcomes, update the model, and measure what improved.

The emphasis is on reproducible experiments and on infrastructure that keeps working as tasks get longer and more complex. The repository already contains model and evaluation components, reasoning experiments, and early RL training work. Long-horizon agent training and distributed RL infrastructure are active development directions.

Experiments

GRPO ablation on MATH-500

Base model64.0%
Step 548.0%
Step 5060.0%

Response length shrank from 303 to about 220 tokens over the run.

What it showed

Accuracy dropped to 48.0% at a 5-step checkpoint before recovering to 60.0% at 50 steps. That is evidence that a sparse binary-correctness reward is a weak signal without more steps and better reward shaping.
