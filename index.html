💬About Me

I am currently building two open-source systems: an Open Post-Training System for advancing open-weight models through reinforcement learning on long-horizon coding and agentic tasks, and an Open Distributed Training System focused on multi-device training, JAX sharding, collective communication, and GPU/TPU performance engineering.

Previously, I worked as a researcher at Carnegie Mellon University on long-horizon agentic systems, studying where models fail across extended coding and agentic tasks and developing evaluation environments and verifiers to identify failures in context retention, state tracking, planning, and execution. Before that, at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), I studied the internal representations associated with reasoning and memorization in language models.

My work brings together reinforcement learning, agent environments and evaluation, distributed systems, and accelerator performance. I share research code, experiments, and technical explanations through GitHub and Substack.
